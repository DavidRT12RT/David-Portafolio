# Surface brief: home

## Scope
The single-page portfolio at `app/page.tsx` and every section it renders. Visitor mode: Experience.

## Audience, job, action
Recruiters and founders/clients, weighted equally. In seconds they must see that David ships production AI systems end to end; the action is writing to `davidarcosm@hotmail.com` or opening LinkedIn/GitHub. Proof is the real work: projects with screenshots, history, stack, process. Constraints: ES/EN copy kept verbatim plus the owner-approved Greek room names; free registry components only; figures are SMK public-domain scans.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The portfolio is a walk through the city of Thomas Cole's The Consummation of Empire (1836) in the afternoon: the painting opens it, and every room after is a terrace of that city where a marble figure, or in ΕΡΓΑ the works themselves under a shaded portico, stands in the haze until the visitor's arrival lets the sun find it. It refuses the developer-portfolio defaults of neon accent, glass cards, bento grid and particle hero.
OWN-WORLD: Owner-revised on 2026-10-19 to the painting's colours: hazy-sky ground #EBE5DD, umber ink #24170F (soft #5A4535, mute #76624F), gilding #B4843F with deep gold #85581F for small text and light gold #D9B06A on shade, sienna #9C4A24 kept for rare emphasis, stone #D3C7B8 for hairlines, sunlit marble #F7F2EA for labels and for text in the portico's shade #21150E. Light and haze are the only gradients; whatever the sun has not reached dissolves into its room's ground. Museum cartelas with real SMK data are the component language. Commissioner, by a Greek type designer with Greek script, for reading and flared room signage; Cormorant SC 600 (the owner's pick) for the name in the painting's sky.
STORY: Painting and figure take turns (owner-requested 2026-10-19): the visitor arrives in Cole's city, meets Plato in ΛΟΓΟΣ, rides Rubens' Battle of the Amazons into ΟΔΥΣΣΕΙΑ where Homer keeps the history, walks Bayeu's Olympus: The Fall of the Giants through ΜΕΘΟΔΟΣ, meets the discus thrower in ΤΕΧΝΗ standing between two vitrines where every technology is struck as a coin, hears Miola's oracle explain how David's AI answers in ΜΑΝΤΕΙΟΝ, sees the works on Ionic columns in ΕΡΓΑ with Church's Parthenon beyond them, watches Jordaens' giants fall under Jupiter's thunderbolts in ΓΙΓΑΝΤΟΜΑΧΙΑ, and is greeted by Apollo in ΧΑΙΡΕ; understands that David builds thinking systems with classical rigour; writes to him.
